Introduction

Hardam Rasul teaches 12th-grade English at Bizhara High School, preparing Wizari students for one of the most important exams of their academic lives. With over a decade in education — and as a tutor at Student Academy — he has guided countless students toward exam success. He is also a mentor at Masterly, a digital space for growth and learning, carrying his approach to teaching beyond the classroom.
